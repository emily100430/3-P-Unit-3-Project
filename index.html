The Best Fudgy Brownies

  • Time: 20-30 minutes
  • Servings: 16
Ingredients Quantity
Unsalted Butter: 1/2 cup
Olive Oil: 1 tbsp
Superfine Sugar: 1 1/8 cup
Large Eggs: 2
Vanilla Extract: 2 tsp
All Purpose Flour: 1/2 cup
Cocoa Powder: 1/2 cup
Salt: 1/4 tsp

Step 1:Combine hot melted butter, oil and sugar together in a medium-sized bowl. Whisk well for about a minute.

Step 2: Add the eggs and vanilla; beat until lighter in color (another minute).

Step 3:Sift in flour, cocoa powder and salt. Gently fold the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients until JUST combined.

Step 4:Pour batter into prepared pan, smoothing the top out evenly.

Step 5:Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, or until the center of the pan no longer jiggles and is just set to the touch. Remove and allow to cool to room temperature before slicing into 16 brownies.
